### Fixed simulation step
- Files: `crates/host/src/uncap.rs` (the fixed-step accumulator in the uncapped `BSTimer` step and the published draw fraction), `crates/host/src/phys_blend.rs` (the draw time when a fraction is published)
- Upstream approach: `00aa4ee0` reports each frame's real elapsed time with no sleep and no 10 ms floor when `NV_UNCAP` is on (`crates/host/src/uncap.rs`, `crates/engine-bs/src/units/bscore/bstimer.rs`); Havok steps 16 ms at a time and `NV_PHYS_BLEND` draws bodies at T - 16 ms. Upstream has no fixed simulation step (checked at the base `c66ef0a8a`, 2026-11-26)
- Fork approach: the main timer carries wall time in an exact 1/60 s accumulator and reports a whole step only when one is due, with zero-second frames between steps. The carried fraction moves the blended draw time on towards the clock on those frames (capped inside Havok's last step); the time past a frame's 166 ms is dropped as in the original, the main timer's `iLastTime` follows the reported steps by their milliseconds, and a resting body is drawn as it is, and the slow-motion ramp does not move without a step. Havok's step and accumulator are unchanged; the other timers and every path with the switch off run as before
- Decision: drop when upstream's own timer reports fixed steps and interpolates the drawing, or when the game's systems no longer change their behaviour with a shorter frame
- Reason: at 144, 300 and 500 frames a second the game's seconds, a jump's height, a landing, the slow-motion ramp and a held body's pull change with the frame rate, even with the companion fixes; the fixed step makes them equal and bounds the clock's error against wall time
- Evidence: nv-rs#916 and nv-rs#933; ADR-0018; `the_fixed_step_keeps_the_clock_at_every_frame_rate` (0.5 % bound at 60, 144, 300 and 500 fps), `the_step_is_the_same_at_every_frame_rate`, `render_frames_carry_the_fraction_of_a_step`, `a_long_pause_does_not_fast_forward`, `a_drawing_only_frame_does_not_move_the_multiplier` and `the_fixed_step_moves_the_draw_time_between_steps`, `the_published_step_fraction_moves_the_drawn_body_forward`, `a_stall_gives_the_capped_steps_and_no_burst_after_it`, `the_last_time_follows_the_reported_steps_smoothly`, `a_stall_closes_the_last_time_gap`, `the_ramp_after_a_second_is_the_same_at_every_frame_rate` and `a_resting_body_is_drawn_as_it_is`
