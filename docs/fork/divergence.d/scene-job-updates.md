### Scene job updates
- Files: `crates/engine-core/src/real_threads.rs` (the scene helper and the helper area's slot layout), `crates/engine-fallout-misc/src/units/fallout_misc/main.rs` (the scene graph update call in `Main::OnIdle`)
- Upstream approach: upstream runs the world scene graph's frame update (the vtable call at `0086e9b5`, `BSSceneGraph::SetViewDistanceBasedOnFrameRate`) inline on the game's main thread, then fixes the camera and multibound visibility and draws. Its one posted-job helper is the drawing helper (`post_draw`), which the world draw owns. Checked in `origin/fork` at `c66ef0a8a`.
- Fork approach: `NV_SCENE_JOBS=1` posts that call to a third helper (`nv-scene`, made when real threads start) and joins before the camera and visibility passes and the AI workers. The animation update (`0086fc60`) stays inline. Stepped frames, diagnostics and dual typed runs keep the inline calls. The seam has no float arithmetic of its own; the typed families the calls reach are `Threads::Both` and follow ADR-0014's f64 locals rounded at each float store.
- Decision: keep, offer back when upstream runs the scene update on its own job helper; then drop the switch and the second helper.
- Reason: the scene graph and animation updates are about 13% of the game main thread's samples (the scene graph part moves here) (`docs/fork/PERF.md`, 2026-11-16), so the uncapped preset needs them off main (nv-rs#916, nv-rs#950).
- Evidence: the `engine-core` tests `scene_jobs_stepped` and `scene_jobs_real`, built from made-up guest memory: a stepped post is refused and the caller does the work; with real threads the job runs beside main, one job at a time, is joined and returns a stop at the join. The stepped recording, race campaign and ABBA gates are left to the maintainer with the commands in `docs/fork/SCENE-JOBS.md`; no game runs were available here.
