### Havok step on a helper thread in stepped runs
- Files: `crates/engine-core/src/lib.rs`, `crates/engine-core/src/real_threads.rs`, `crates/engine-fallout-ai/src/units/fallout_ai/run_009ba550.rs`, `crates/engine-fallout-cell/src/units/fallout_shared/run_004a3c20.rs`, `crates/engine-fallout-cell/src/units/fallout_shared/tesobjectrefr_p3.rs`, `crates/engine-fallout-env/src/units/fallout_shared_havok/havokstream.rs`, `crates/engine-fallout-forms/src/units/fallout_shared/globalfunc.rs`, `crates/engine-fallout-magic/src/units/fallout_magic/bhktelekinesislistener.rs`, `crates/engine-fallout-unplaced-b/src/units/unplaced/run_00cb75d0.rs`, `crates/engine-havok/src/units/bshavok/bhkworld.rs`, `crates/jip/src/actor_hooks.rs`
- Upstream approach: `bhkWorld::Update` calls `WORLD_STEP_DELTA_TIME` or `WORLD_STEP_MULTITHREADED` with `e.call` on the game thread, and the translated call sites reach Havok fields directly, so every stepped run, recording and trace has the step and those reads on that thread; checked in the translation at 00c6ae70 (the loop up to `WORLD_STEP_DONE`) and in the call sites above
- Fork approach: `bhk` is the only way code outside Havok reaches a Havok field, and the two update calls go through it. With stepped threads and no diagnostic on, the step is posted to an `nv-havok` OS thread and its poster joins at once, with the poster's x87 control word and virtual clock; with real threads, a diagnostic or a refused post it stays on the calling thread. The step keeps its place in the call order: a stop inside it unwinds at the join with the helper's stopped calls put back on the poster's stack, so the stop report is the same as for an inline call.
- Decision: keep; drop when upstream provides the `bhk` seam itself or its own component-thread step
- Reason: nv-rs#944 puts Havok on its own thread first in stepped mode, where a byte-identical recording can prove the move before the race campaign for real threads
- Evidence: `crates/engine-core/tests/bhk_thread_stepped.rs` compares the helper's step with an inline one (same words, a control word taken from the poster, same virtual clock, no job left posted, a diagnostic refusal, and the same stop text and call stack); `crates/engine-core/tests/bhk_seam.rs` checks the census's Havok fields are reached only through `bhk`; the existing `bhkworld` update tests pass with the diagnostics that keep the step inline. The stepped recording comparison is left to the maintainer with the commands in docs/fork/HAVOK-THREAD.md; no game runs were available here
