### Havok step on a helper thread in stepped runs
- Files: `crates/engine-havok/src/units/bshavok/bhkworld.rs` (the two step calls in `bhk_world_update` go through `engine_core::bhk::step`)
- Upstream approach: `bhkWorld::Update` calls `WORLD_STEP_DELTA_TIME` or `WORLD_STEP_MULTITHREADED` with `e.call` on the game thread, so every stepped run, recording and trace has the step on that thread; checked in the translation at 00c6ae70 (the loop up to `WORLD_STEP_DONE`)
- Fork approach: the two calls go through the `bhk` seam. With stepped threads and no diagnostic on, the step runs on an `nv-havok` OS thread and its poster joins at once, with the poster's x87 control word and virtual clock; with real threads, a diagnostic or a refused post it stays on the calling thread. Only the step moves: the calls around it keep their order and arguments
- Decision: keep; drop when upstream provides the `bhk` seam itself or its own component-thread step
- Reason: nv-rs#944 puts Havok on its own thread first in stepped mode, where a byte-identical recording can prove the move before the race campaign for real threads
- Evidence: `crates/engine-core/tests/bhk_thread_stepped.rs` compares the helper's step with an inline one (same words, same control word, same virtual clock, no job left posted); `crates/engine-core/tests/bhk_seam.rs` checks the census's Havok fields are reached only through `bhk`; the existing `bhkworld` update tests pass with the diagnostics that keep the step inline. The stepped recording comparison is left to the maintainer with the commands in docs/fork/HAVOK-THREAD.md; no game runs were available here
