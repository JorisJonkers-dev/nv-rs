### Far lights at night
- Files: `crates/engine-fallout-env/src/units/fallout_shared_distant_terrain_system/bgsdistantobjectblock.rs` (`far_lights`, `is_far_lights_shape`, `prepare_far_lights`, `far_lights_frame`, the prepare `006f66e0`), `crates/engine-fallout-env/src/units/fallout_shared_sky/sky.rs` (`sky_update`), `crates/fork-gfx/src/builtin.rs`, `crates/host/src/fork_graphics.rs`
- Upstream approach: a far-object block's later shapes are prepared as far buildings (fork: every leaf shape; the exe: only the first)
- Fork approach: a shape carrying extra data named `FarLights` is left out of that prepare, given its own shader and hidden; under the graphics setting `light.far_lights` (live, off by default and under `original` and `performance`, on under `modern`) it is shown by night with its shader property's alpha at a night factor from the climate's sunrise and sunset hours; a shape (one per cell, at the cell's centre) whose cell the game has loaded is hidden, looked at every tenth frame, since the real lamps of that cell are drawn
- Decision: keep
- Reason: the maintainer wants street lamps visible from far at night, small and soft, general for any far-view tool's output; by day nothing may show
- Evidence: `the_night_factor_follows_the_climate_s_sunrise_and_sunset`, `a_far_lights_shape_is_marked_by_its_extra_data_name`, `a_far_lights_shape_keeps_its_own_properties_and_is_hidden_while_the_setting_is_off`, `far_lights_are_shown_by_night_and_the_blocks_are_visited_only_when_the_factor_changes`, `the_marker_check_reads_at_most_64_extra_data_objects`, `a_far_lights_shape_in_a_loaded_cell_is_left_out_and_comes_back_when_the_cell_unloads`, `far_lights_are_on_in_modern_and_off_otherwise_unless_chosen`; night and day pictures (pull request)
