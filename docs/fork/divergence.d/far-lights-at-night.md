### Far lights at night
- Files: `crates/engine-fallout-env/src/units/fallout_shared_distant_terrain_system/bgsdistantobjectblock.rs` (`far_lights`, `is_far_lights_shape`, `prepare_far_lights`, `far_lights_frame`, the prepare `006f66e0`), `crates/engine-fallout-env/src/units/fallout_shared_sky/sky.rs` (`sky_update`), `crates/fork-gfx/src/builtin.rs`, `crates/host/src/fork_graphics.rs`
- Upstream approach: a far-object block's later shapes are prepared as far buildings (fork: every leaf shape; the exe: only the first)
- Fork approach: a shape carrying extra data named `FarLights` is left out of that prepare, given its own shader and hidden; under the graphics setting `light.far_lights` (live, off by default, under `original` and `modern`) it is shown by night with its shader property's alpha at a night factor from the climate's sunrise and sunset hours
- Decision: keep
- Reason: the maintainer wants street lamps visible from far at night, small and soft, general for any far-view tool's output; by day nothing may show
- Evidence: `the_night_factor_follows_the_climate_s_sunrise_and_sunset`, `a_far_lights_shape_is_marked_by_its_extra_data_name`, `a_far_lights_shape_keeps_its_own_properties_and_is_hidden_while_the_setting_is_off`, `far_lights_are_shown_by_night_and_the_blocks_are_visited_only_when_the_factor_changes`, `far_lights_are_off_unless_chosen`; night and day pictures (pull request)
