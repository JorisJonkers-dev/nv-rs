### Late AI jobs
- Files: `crates/engine-core/src/lib.rs` (the module declaration), `crates/engine-core/src/late_ai.rs` (the seam), `crates/engine-fallout-misc/src/units/fallout_misc/main.rs` (the `0086f6a0` body and its call)
- Upstream approach: `Main::UpdateNonRenderSafeAITasks` runs its process-list, Pip-Boy and task-queue calls on the game's main thread after `WaitForThreads`. Its only posted-job helper is the drawing helper (`post_draw`), which owns the world draw; checked in `origin/fork` at `64f8101cc`.
- Fork approach: `NV_LATE_AI=1` passes the `0086f6a0` body to one worker and joins before the call returns. With stepped threads the body is posted to the stepped helper, which takes the poster's thread identity, x87 control word and virtual clock and starts at the join; with real threads one helper of the existing pool runs it while main waits. A diagnostic, a typed family in dual mode, a stepped helper already holding a job or unshareable memory leave the body inline. The switch is off in every preset. The seam adds no float arithmetic: the process family's level passes, which hold its `f32` subtraction and set its `Threads::Main` row, are not called here; the after-draw functions called here move floats as words, and the bone update they reach already runs on helpers under `NV_PAR_BONES`.
- Decision: keep, offer back when upstream moves these tasks to a worker with the same joined dependency; then drop the switch and the seam.
- Reason: the late AI tasks are a named part of the frame tail in `docs/FIXES.md`, and #916 asks for them off the game thread so the uncapped preset can reach its frame-time target (nv-rs#940).
- Evidence: `crates/engine-core/tests/late_ai_stepped.rs` compares the posted and inline bodies' calls, words, x87 word, virtual clock, guest bytes and stop stack from stand-ins built here; `crates/engine-core/tests/late_ai_real.rs` checks that with real threads the body runs on a helper and that a diagnostic keeps it inline. The stepped recording, race campaign and ABBA gates are left to the maintainer with the commands in `docs/fork/LATE-AI-JOBS.md`; no game runs were available here.
