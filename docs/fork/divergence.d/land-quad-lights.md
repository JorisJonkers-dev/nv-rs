### More lights on a land quad
- Files: `crates/engine-core/src/land_lights.rs` (the switch), `crates/engine-bs/src/units/bsshader/bsshaderpplightingproperty_passes_2x.rs` (`AddLandscapePasses_2x`, `00bdf3e0`), `crates/engine-typed/src/draw/land_extra.rs` and `crates/engine-typed/src/shader/batch.rs` (the draw of an extra pass), `crates/engine-typed/src/draw/setups.rs`, `crates/engine/src/lib.rs`, `crates/fork-gfx/src/builtin.rs`, `crates/fork-gfx/presets/modern.toml`, `crates/host/src/fork_graphics.rs`
- Upstream approach: a land quad takes the first three active non-shadow lights that are not black, in `ResortLights` order, for its one lit pass (`LANDnO4`, the sun and those three); the others do not light that ground
- Fork approach: under the graphics setting `light.land_lights` (live; off by default and under `original` and `performance`, on under `modern`) the walk goes on to the twelfth lit light and each further three get a pass of the same land type that is not first (sort byte 0). The batch renderer draws such a pass added to the picture, z-equal; the fork sets blend one-one for it, and zeroes what the land pixel shader adds in every pass: the ambient colour (pixel constant 1), the sun's colour (pixel constant 3) and the fog colour (vertex constant 15, which the pixel shader blends in by the fog factor), so the pass adds only its three point lights. The first pass after them puts the fog colour back. With three lights or fewer, or the setting off, nothing changes: the passes are the exe's, entry for entry
- Decision: keep
- Reason: with the longer lamp reach (`light.lamp_distance`) many lamps and fires stand within reach of a quad, and the game's three lights leave the ground under the rest dark (nv-rs#912). The game has no lights-only land pass type (the 56 types 0x1f7 to 0x22e are seven texture counts, with or without the four lights, two flags), and the shader (`SLS2100` to `SLS2147`) ends each pass as `(lights + ambient) * texture` fogged: `r0 = lights + AmbientColor`, `r0 = r0 * texture * vertex colour`, `result = t7.w * (t7.xyz - r0) + r0` with `t7` the vertex shader's fog colour and factor, so a second pass of it adds the ambient and the fog colour again
- Evidence: `land_lights_extra_passes_take_the_lights_past_the_third_three_to_a_pass`, `the_choice_follows_the_sort_byte_and_the_setting`, `an_extra_pass_zeroes_the_ambient_the_sun_and_once_the_fog_and_a_first_pass_puts_it_back`, `land_lights_are_on_in_modern_and_off_otherwise_unless_chosen`; night pictures and mean luma (pull request)
