### Native float maths picks the x87's NaN, not the optimiser's
- Files: `crates/engine-typed/src/collision/body.rs`, `crates/engine-typed/src/dispatch/ray.rs`, `crates/engine-typed/src/extra_data/records.rs`, `crates/engine-typed/src/facegen/apply.rs`, `crates/engine-typed/src/facegen/keyframe.rs`, `crates/engine-typed/src/lights/scene_frustum.rs`, `crates/engine-typed/src/lights/scene_light.rs`, `crates/engine-typed/src/native.rs`, `crates/engine-typed/src/particles/modifiers.rs`, `crates/engine-typed/src/particles/system.rs`, `crates/engine-typed/src/pathing/navmesh.rs`, `crates/engine-typed/src/pathing/point.rs`, `crates/engine-typed/src/pathing/pov.rs`, `crates/engine-typed/src/pathing/search.rs`, `crates/engine-typed/src/scene_node/node.rs`, `crates/engine-fallout-cell/src/units/fallout_shared/extradatalist.rs`
- Upstream approach: `native::Real` is `f32` and `f64`, and the rotation matrix works in `f64`; an operation with a NaN operand keeps the first operand's NaN on SSE, and the optimiser may swap the operands of a commutative operation. The luminance Diffs accept payload differences (`allow_nan_payloads`); the light queue Diff and `xd_rotation_matrix` do not, and differ in one payload byte when engine-typed is built at opt-level 1.
- Fork approach: `native::Real` is implemented by `Single` and `Double`, newtypes whose operators give the x87's NaN (`engine_core::x87`: the one NaN quieted, of two the quiet one, else the larger significand) when an operand is a NaN and the host's result otherwise; the rotation matrix computes over `Double`. The instantiations of the generic functions name the new types (`::<Single>`, `::<Double>`).
- Decision: keep; drop when upstream makes its native float code's NaN choice explicit, or when its Diffs accept NaN payloads
- Reason: the payload of a NaN result is the original game's at every optimisation level, so the typed CI jobs build engine-typed at opt-level 1 without an exception, and no Diff is loosened
- Evidence: nv-rs#1009; `typed_lights` and `typed_cell_refs` pass at opt-level 0, 1 and 3
